Terms & Conditions

1) General

"Bin Sharida" is an individual activity run by its owner under a valid freelance work permit (وثيقة عمل حر), not a registered company or establishment — these terms govern dealings between him and any customer requesting products or services through the website or direct contact, whether a one-time order or a recurring supply contract.

Using the website or submitting a quote request is considered implicit agreement to these terms.

2) Ordering & Pricing

No final fixed prices are displayed on the website — every quote is determined by product type, quantity, and order frequency (one-time or recurring contract).

A quote sent to a customer is valid for a limited period (stated when the quote is sent) and may change afterward based on stock and import/shipping costs from suppliers.

For recurring supply contracts, Bin Sharida commits to notifying the customer in writing via WhatsApp of any price change at least 48 hours before the next shipment; no price change applies retroactively to shipments already agreed in writing.

The minimum order is one carton. Wholesale pricing is calculated for orders of 10 cartons or more, based on product, quantity, and agreed terms.

An order is confirmed once the customer gives written approval (via WhatsApp or email) of the quote sent.

3) Payment

For recurring supply contracts, the customer chooses one of two payment methods, agreed in writing at contracting:

  • Immediate payment: paying for each shipment in cash or via Mada on delivery, or within 24 hours of receiving a consolidated invoice for a prior supply cycle.
  • Prepaid balance (sustaining package): topping up a cash balance in advance, automatically debited per quantity received, with a commitment to top up again once the balance reaches an agreed minimum.

For one-time orders, payment is made via bank transfer to Bin Sharida's business bank account, or another method agreed in writing.

Bin Sharida is not currently registered for VAT, and its activity does not exceed the mandatory registration threshold (SAR 375,000 annually) set by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — accordingly, no VAT is added to agreed prices.

Should the business later exceed the statutory registration threshold, this policy will be updated and disclosed to customers immediately.

4) Delivery

Free delivery is available to Taybah, Al Shaulah, Al Nada, Al Fakhria, Al Faisaliyah, Al Amal, Al Manar, Al Waha, and Hajar.

Delivery to other locations is arranged by agreement and quantity. Pickup can be arranged at the nearest agreed point within Taybah District, coordinated with the customer when confirming the order.

Delivery timing is confirmed with the order based on quantity and location.

5) Returns & Exchanges

Unused products in original condition may be returned within 3 days of receipt in case of an order error or a manufacturing defect that prevents the printer from reading the paper.

Opened quantities or items damaged due to improper storage by the customer after receipt are not eligible for return. Returned cartons must be sealed in their original condition, except for a single roll that may be opened for inspection.

Any exchange or return is coordinated in advance via direct contact (WhatsApp).

6) Cancelling & Suspending Recurring Contracts

Either party may cancel a recurring supply contract with written notice via WhatsApp or email, at least 7 business days before the next shipment. Upon cancellation, any remaining prepaid balance is refunded to the customer after deducting the value of the last shipment actually delivered.

If the customer falls behind on a due invoice or their prepaid balance runs out, Bin Sharida may suspend recurring delivery after notifying the customer in writing with at least 48 hours' notice, and is not liable for any impact on the customer's operations resulting from that suspension.

7) Thermal Paper Storage Responsibility

The customer must store thermal paper rolls in a dry, cool place (below 25°C), away from direct sunlight, moisture, and heat from kitchen equipment. Bin Sharida is not liable for any damage or print fading resulting from improper storage after receipt.

8) Liability & Force Majeure

The owner is committed to matching products to the specifications agreed at the time of order.

Because thermal paper materials depend on global supply chains, any delay beyond Bin Sharida's control (international shipping crises, raw material shortages, weather conditions) is considered an exceptional circumstance — Bin Sharida commits to notifying the customer immediately and providing available alternatives with the same fixed specifications where possible, with no penalties owed by either party.
