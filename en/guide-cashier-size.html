Buyer's Guide — Cashier Paper

How to Choose the Right Cashier Roll Size

The two common sizes in Saudi Arabia are 80mm and 57mm — the wrong one causes jamming or garbled printing. Here is how to tell them apart in two minutes.

Why size matters

Your POS or cashier device is built for one exact paper width. A roll that is too wide or too narrow will jam the print head, print at an angle, or wear down the feed roller over time. Size is a technical spec that must match your device exactly, not a cosmetic detail.

80mm vs 57mm

80mm is the most common size for fixed cashier machines in shops, restaurants, and supermarkets — the wider format fits receipts with more line items, a logo, or a barcode. Most fixed POS terminals in Saudi Arabia are built for this size.

57mm is used in portable and compact devices, and some kitchen order printers. It is also close to the size used by Mada/Visa payment terminals, though usually with a different roll core diameter — which is why we treat it as a separate product (see our Mada vs Cashier guide).

How to check your device's exact size

  • Open the roll compartment and measure the old roll's width with a ruler — don't estimate by eye
  • Check the device manual or the spec label on its base — it usually lists the width in millimeters
  • If the device accepts more than one core diameter, check that too (usually 40-70mm), not just the width
  • If you have several different devices in one shop, check each one — they aren't always the same size

A common mistake

The most common issue we see: a shop orders "thermal paper" without specifying a size, receives 80mm, and discovers their device needs 57mm. That's why we always confirm the size before supplying, and don't sell 58mm at all to avoid confusion with 57 and 80 — a single millimeter difference causes real compatibility problems.
