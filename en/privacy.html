Privacy Policy

1) Data we collect

When you fill in a "Get a Quote" request or contact us directly via WhatsApp, we may collect: your name, business name, phone number, email (if provided), delivery address, and order details (product type, quantity, notes).

We do not collect any payment details or bank account numbers through the website — payment is arranged separately and directly after agreeing on a quote.

2) How we use your data

We use your data only to contact you about your request, prepare a quote, and coordinate delivery. We do not use it for any marketing purpose unrelated to your request without your consent.

3) How we protect your data

The quote request form on this website sends your details directly into a WhatsApp conversation with us — your data is not stored on any server or database belonging to the website.

WhatsApp conversations are end-to-end encrypted by WhatsApp itself.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for marketing purposes.

We keep contact and order data only for as long as needed to complete the transaction and handle any warranty or return.

4) Your rights

You may ask us at any time to delete your data or stop contacting you, by messaging us directly on WhatsApp.

5) Contact about privacy

For any question about this policy or your data, contact us on WhatsApp: 0591342020.

6) Changes to this policy

Bin Sharida may update this privacy policy at any time; continued dealings after an update constitute acceptance of the new version.
