Mada Thermal Paper

Chinese thermal paper for Mada/Visa payment terminals — size 57×40 mm, 100 rolls per carton. Specifications come first; the brand may vary by availability.

Overview

Mada rolls are a fixed, specific size — they need to match your payment terminal exactly or printing jams and cuts off. Bin Sharida supplies the fixed 57x40mm size and guarantees recurring supply instead of you waiting until it runs out. (Looking for regular cashier rolls? See Cashier Thermal Paper)
