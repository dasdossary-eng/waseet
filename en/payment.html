Payment Policy

Payment is arranged directly after reviewing the quote and confirming the order. The website does not collect card or bank details or charge you automatically.

1) No online payment

Submitting a quote request does not create an immediate payment obligation. We confirm the product, quantity, delivery arrangements, and final price with you before the order is accepted.

2) Available payment methods

  • Cash or Mada on delivery: where agreed, payment can be made to the delivery representative when the order is received.
  • Bank transfer: after the order is reviewed and an invoice or payment details are provided directly.

3) Recurring supply

For recurring supply contracts, the payment schedule and any advance balance arrangement must be agreed in writing before the contract begins.

4) Prices and VAT

Product prices and any delivery charges are confirmed in the quote. Bin Sharida is not currently registered for VAT, so VAT is not added to current agreed prices.

5) Questions

For payment questions, contact us through WhatsApp 0591342020 or binsharida@outlook.sa.
