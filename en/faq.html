Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions — if you don't find yours, message us on WhatsApp.

What's the difference between Mada paper and Cashier thermal paper?

Chinese Mada thermal paper is 57×40 mm with 100 rolls per carton. Chinese cashier thermal paper is 80×70 mm with 50 rolls per carton. Specifications come first; the brand may vary by availability. Full details in our Mada vs Cashier guide.

How long does delivery take, and what's the order cutoff?

Free delivery is available to Taybah, Al Shaulah, An Nada, Al Fakhria, Al Faisaliyah, Al Amal, Al Manar, Al Waha, and Hajar. Other locations are by agreement and quantity; delivery timing is confirmed with the order. Pickup from the nearest agreed point within Taybah District is also available.

Is there a minimum order?

The minimum order is one carton. Wholesale pricing is calculated for orders of 10 cartons or more, based on quantity and agreed terms.

Are your prices VAT-inclusive?

No, Bin Sharida is not currently registered for VAT since its activity doesn't exceed the mandatory registration threshold — no VAT is added to our listed prices.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Free delivery is available to Taybah, Al Shaulah, An Nada, Al Fakhria, Al Faisaliyah, Al Amal, Al Manar, Al Waha, and Hajar. Other locations are by agreement and quantity. Pickup can be arranged at the nearest agreed point within Taybah District.

Do you offer recurring supply contracts instead of one-time orders?

Yes, we offer monthly or quarterly contracts with a fixed quantity matched to your actual consumption. See our Business Solutions page.

Do you sell wholesale or accept distributors?

Yes, we have a distribution partnership program for those who want to buy wholesale and resell in their area. See our Partners page.

What shipping label sizes are available?

We supply sizes compatible with common courier systems in Saudi Arabia. See our shipping label sizes guide for details, and we confirm the exact size with you before supplying.

How can I place an order?

The easiest way is WhatsApp at 0591342020, or fill in the quote request form on any product page and we'll get in touch.

Do you accept returns if a product arrives damaged or wrong?

Yes, we accept returns within 3 days of receipt in case of an order error or product defect. Full details in our Returns Policy.
